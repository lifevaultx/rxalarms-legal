What RxAlarms actually does for you

Most medication apps ask you to do the hard part yourself — typing in every drug name, dose, and schedule by hand. RxAlarms does the opposite. Point your camera at a bottle, upload a document, or just say the medication out loud, and it's tracked. Here's what that unlocks.

Scan it, upload it. Done.

Snap a photo of a pill bottle or prescription, or upload a picture or document of one, and RxAlarms reads the name, dose, and schedule straight off it — no typing, no guessing, no manual setup.

Hands full? Just say it.

Speak the medication name out loud and RxAlarms adds it for you. Built for the moments when you can't stop to type — juggling kids, driving, or just too tired to fuss with a form.

Reminders that don't let you forget

No generic "take your pill" alert. RxAlarms tells you exactly which medication and dose is due, and keeps reminding until you actually confirm it — not until you swipe it away.

Refills tracked automatically

RxAlarms counts down your supply as you log each dose, so you know a refill is coming before you're down to your last pill — not after.

Managing medications for a parent or family member too? RxAlarms keeps everyone's schedules straight and can share a simple adherence summary with whoever else is helping out.
